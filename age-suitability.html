Age suitability for Project Milepost.

Suitability summary

General lifestyle and productivity app. User-created milestone text and images are controlled by the user; the bundled history content is informational.

Advertising and purchases

One Google AdMob banner may be shown only after configuration and consent gates pass. Build 68 forces Google Limited Ads before SDK startup and disables publisher personalization, publisher first-party ID, and Google Mobile Ads SDK exception reporting.

No in-app purchases, subscriptions, paid content, or ad-removal purchase path are offered in this release.

Content and interaction

  • Does not require a Project Milepost account.
  • Does not run a developer server for personal event records.
  • Does not offer in-app purchases, paid content, subscriptions, or ad removal in this release.
  • Does not include advertising or purchase controls inside the Home Screen widget.
