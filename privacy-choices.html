Privacy Choices · A0000007

Privacy choices for Project Milepost.

The controls below use the same structure for every app subsection so users can quickly understand what can be changed, deleted, avoided, or reported.

Local data

  • Delete events, notes, and local photos in the app or delete the app to remove app-local data, subject to normal iOS backup behavior.
  • Use Menu > Ads > Ad Privacy Choices for available advertising privacy controls.
  • Use Menu > Ads > Report an Ad to report inappropriate or age-inappropriate advertising.
  • Do not use Calendar import or local event photos if you do not want the app to access those user-selected sources.

Network and services

  • Google Mobile Ads and Google UMP may process coarse location, device identifiers, advertising data, product interaction, and performance data for limited banner advertising and related service purposes.
  • Build 68 forces Google Limited Ads and disables publisher personalization, publisher first-party ID, and Google Mobile Ads SDK exception reporting.
  • Calendar and photo information is accessed through Apple system permissions only when the user chooses those import or selection workflows.
  • Support email and ordinary website server logs are handled by the relevant email and hosting providers.

Advertising posture

Open Menu > Ads > Ad Privacy Choices to review Google-provided choices when available. Limited Ads remain enforced before SDK startup. The five Google advertising data categories disclosed in App Store Connect are not marked as used to track users, and the app does not request App Tracking Transparency authorization.

Support contact

Email trentshurts@gmail.com with privacy questions.
