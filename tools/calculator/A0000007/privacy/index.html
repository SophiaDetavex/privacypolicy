Project Milepost Privacy Policy

Project Milepost keeps milestone records and selected media locally or in its shared app-group container, accesses Calendar and Photos only when chosen, and integrates one consent-gated Google AdMob banner using Google Limited Ads without Apple-defined cross-app tracking.

1. Information kept on the device

  • Milestone titles, dates, notes, categories, completion state, recurrence settings, and presentation preferences.
  • Photos the user deliberately selects for a milestone and generated local thumbnails.
  • Widget snapshots and app-group state needed to show selected milestone information in the widget extension.

2. Information that may be processed outside the app

  • Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform may process IP-address-derived coarse location, device identifiers, advertising data, product interaction, and performance data for limited banner ad delivery, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and service performance. These are the five data categories disclosed for this release.
  • Build 68 disables Google Mobile Ads SDK exception reporting, publisher personalization, and publisher first-party ID, and forces Google Limited Ads before SDK startup.
  • The developer does not receive the app's milestone text, notes, Calendar records, selected photos, or widget content through the advertising integration. Google and its advertising partners process their own service data under their applicable terms and privacy disclosures.
  • Calendar access occurs through Apple's EventKit framework only after permission. Calendar data is read or written only for user-invoked milestone/calendar actions.
  • Photo access occurs through Apple system pickers or the granted photo-library permission when the user deliberately selects media.
  • Support email and ordinary website hosting logs are handled by the relevant providers only when those services are used.

3. Advertising, consent, and tracking

Google UMP consent information is refreshed before an eligible ad request, and the banner lane collapses when advertising is definitively unavailable or consent is revoked. Users can open Ad Privacy Choices from the app menu when Google provides a privacy-options form.

The App Store privacy disclosure lists Coarse Location, Device ID, Product Interaction, Advertising Data, and Performance Data as collected by the Google advertising integration, but none is marked as used to track users across other companies' apps or websites. Build 68 contains no first-party IDFA access or ATT request path and does not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt.

4. Permissions and protected resources

  • Calendar/full calendar access: optional import or export of milestone events after an explicit system grant.
  • Photo Library: optional user-selected milestone imagery after an explicit system grant or picker action.

5. Retention and deletion

App-local records remain until the user deletes them with an available app control, resets the applicable feature, or removes the app, subject to normal device backups and exported copies. Service providers retain data under their own policies and legal obligations. The developer cannot directly erase provider-controlled records that it does not possess; users should use the provider, device, and in-app privacy controls described here.

6. Your choices

  • Create, edit, complete, archive, or delete milestone records inside the app.
  • Open Ad Privacy Choices in the app to review or change Google consent choices when available.
  • Decline Calendar or Photos permission and continue using milestone features that do not need them.
  • Remove the app and its widgets to delete app-local and app-group data, subject to normal iOS backup behavior.

7. What the reviewed release does not do

  • No Project Milepost account or developer-hosted milestone cloud is required.
  • Milestone text and selected photos are not supplied to Google for ad targeting through the app's integration.
  • The app does not provide public profiles, messaging, or a social feed.
  • Calendar and photo access are not used before the user grants the relevant permission or chooses an item.

8. Children and age suitability

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children through an account or developer backend. Parents and guardians should supervise device permissions, purchases, and service-provider consent as appropriate. See the age-suitability page.

9. Security

The app relies on iOS sandboxing, Apple permission controls, and ordinary platform protections. No software or storage method can guarantee absolute security. Keep iOS current, protect the device passcode, and avoid including sensitive information in support email unless it is necessary.

10. International and provider processing

Apple, Google, email, hosting, and other selected service providers may process data in jurisdictions where they operate. Their own privacy notices govern their processing. The app's first-party local content is not thereby converted into developer-hosted cloud content.

11. Policy changes

A materially changed app or SDK configuration requires this policy and applicable App Store privacy answers to be reviewed before release. The revision date below identifies this published version.
