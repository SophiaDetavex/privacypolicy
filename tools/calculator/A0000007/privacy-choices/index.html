Advertising privacy choices

The free release integrates one consent-gated Google AdMob banner. Build 68 forces Google Limited Ads before SDK startup, disables publisher personalization, publisher first-party ID, and Google Mobile Ads SDK exception reporting, and does not request App Tracking Transparency authorization.

  1. Open Project Milepost.
  2. Open the Menu.
  3. Open the Ads section and select Ad Privacy Choices.
  4. Review or change the choices that Google makes available for the user's region.

A control may report that privacy options are not currently required or available. That status can depend on region, consent configuration, network state, and Google UMP. Revoking consent removes the banner when Google no longer permits ad requests, but it does not necessarily erase data already retained by Google or an advertising partner under its own policy.

The App Store disclosure lists Coarse Location, Device ID, Product Interaction, Advertising Data, and Performance Data as collected for the Google advertising integration, but none is marked as used to track users across other companies' apps or websites.

Local data controls

  • Create, edit, complete, archive, or delete milestone records inside the app.
  • Open Ad Privacy Choices in the app to review or change Google consent choices when available.
  • Decline Calendar or Photos permission and continue using milestone features that do not need them.
  • Remove the app and its widgets to delete app-local and app-group data, subject to normal iOS backup behavior.

iOS permission controls

Protected-resource access can be reviewed in the iOS Settings app. Disabling a permission prevents future access but does not automatically delete content the user previously exported or data retained by an external provider.

  • Calendar/full calendar access: optional import or export of milestone events after an explicit system grant.
  • Photo Library: optional user-selected milestone imagery after an explicit system grant or picker action.

Support and deletion requests

Because the reviewed app does not require a developer account or upload its private workspace to a developer backend, the developer generally has no server-side app account to delete. For a privacy question or suspected discrepancy, email trentshurts@gmail.com and avoid sending private app content unless necessary.
