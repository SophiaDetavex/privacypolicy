Age suitability · A0000007

Age suitability for Project Milepost

A content profile supporting App Store age-rating review; the App Store rating remains controlled by the submitted questionnaire and binary.

No public chatNo real-money wageringNo unrestricted browsing

Content profile

  • The app supplies organizational utility content and does not include public user-generated content or communication.
  • It does not include gambling, mature media, unrestricted browsing, or commerce directed at children.
  • Final App Store age-rating answers must match the submitted binary and current Apple questionnaire.

Privacy and permissions

Project Milepost keeps milestone records and selected media locally or in its shared app-group container, accesses Calendar and Photos only when chosen, and integrates one consent-gated Google AdMob banner using Google Limited Ads without Apple-defined cross-app tracking.

  • Calendar/full calendar access: optional import or export of milestone events after an explicit system grant.
  • Photo Library: optional user-selected milestone imagery after an explicit system grant or picker action.

Parents and guardians

Use Apple family, purchase, permission, and Screen Time controls appropriate for the device user. Service-provider consent and advertising controls, where present, may differ by region.
