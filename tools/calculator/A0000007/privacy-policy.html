Project Milepost Privacy Policy

1. Information stored locally

  • Event names, dates, notes, bundled-history selections, settings, and timeline preferences.
  • Selected Calendar event information imported by the user.
  • App-optimized local copies of selected event photos.
  • Widget snapshot information for the selected local milestone.

2. What may leave the device

  • Google AdMob and Google UMP may process information needed to provide, limit, secure, measure, and improve compact banner advertising where privacy and consent conditions allow ad requests.
  • Calendar and photo information is accessed through Apple system permissions only when the user chooses those import or selection workflows.
  • Support email and ordinary website server logs are handled by the relevant email and hosting providers.

3. Advertising, consent, and purchases

Google AdMob compact top banner advertising is disclosed for this release.

No in-app purchases, subscriptions, paid content, or ad-removal purchase path are offered in this release.

4. What this app does not do

  • Does not require a Project Milepost account.
  • Does not run a developer server for personal event records.
  • Does not offer in-app purchases, paid content, subscriptions, or ad removal in this release.
  • Does not include advertising or purchase controls inside the Home Screen widget.

5. Your choices

  • Delete events, notes, and local photos in the app or delete the app to remove app-local data, subject to normal iOS backup behavior.
  • Use Menu > Ads > Ad Privacy Choices for available advertising privacy controls.
  • Use Menu > Ads > Report an Ad to report inappropriate or age-inappropriate advertising.
  • Do not use Calendar import or local event photos if you do not want the app to access those user-selected sources.

6. Contact

For privacy or support questions, email trentshurts@gmail.com.

Change-control requirement

This policy describes the current source package posture. Update this policy, App Store privacy answers, App Review notes, and in-app disclosures before release if app behavior changes.
