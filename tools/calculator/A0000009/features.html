Project Game Room features.

An offline-first mini-game collection with Tic-tac-toe, Chess, Checkers, and Words and Letters visible in the current build. Sudoku and Air Hockey are dormant and hidden by feature flags.

Offline-first4 visible mini-gamesNo active adsSudoku + Air Hockey dormant

Current visible games

Tic-tac-toe, Chess, Checkers, and Words and Letters are visible and launchable in the current build.

Dormant games retained

Sudoku and Air Hockey remain in source for future reactivation, but they are hidden from the carousel and refused by launch routing while disabled.

Local profiles and progress

Guest play is available, and user-created profile names, visual preferences, sound settings, and supported progress stay on device.

Offline posture

The current build does not require login, purchases, chat, active advertising, or network gameplay.
