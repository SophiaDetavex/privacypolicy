1. Information stored locally

  • Local profile names, active profile, theme, color, sound, and carousel preferences.
  • Game configuration choices such as difficulty, side, background, or local play options.
  • Local progress, saved game state, and best-score information for supported games.
  • Words and Letters progress and first-party vocabulary state needed for gameplay.

2. What may leave the device

  • Support email and ordinary website server logs are handled by the relevant email and hosting providers.

3. Advertising, consent, and purchases

Advertising-readiness source is present, but ads are disabled by default and Google advertising packages are not linked in the current archive.

No in-app purchases, subscriptions, casino mechanics, wagering, or ad removal are offered in this release.

4. What this app does not do

  • Does not require a Project Game Room account.
  • Does not include login, purchases, subscriptions, chat, public profiles, gambling, wagering, casino economy, chips, or betting mechanics.
  • Does not actively initialize Google Mobile Ads, preload ads, request ads, show ad views, or show advertising consent prompts while ads are disabled.
  • Does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, Bluetooth, health, or calendar permissions in the current build.
  • Does not expose Sudoku or Air Hockey to users while their feature flags are disabled.

5. Your choices

  • Use local settings to adjust profiles, colors, sound, and supported gameplay preferences.
  • Delete local app data or delete the app to remove app-local profiles and progress, subject to normal iOS backup behavior.
  • If advertising is enabled in a future build, the website, App Store privacy answers, and in-app privacy choices must be updated before release.

6. Contact

For privacy or support questions, email trentshurts@gmail.com.

Change-control requirement

This policy describes the current source package posture. Update this policy, App Store privacy answers, App Review notes, and in-app disclosures before release if app behavior changes.
