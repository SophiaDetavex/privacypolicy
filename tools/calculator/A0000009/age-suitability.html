Suitability summary

Casual board and word games for general audiences. No gambling, wagering, chat, public profiles, or user-generated content sharing is provided.

Advertising and purchases

Advertising-readiness source is present, but ads are disabled by default and Google advertising packages are not linked in the current archive.

No in-app purchases, subscriptions, casino mechanics, wagering, or ad removal are offered in this release.

Content and interaction

  • Does not require a Project Game Room account.
  • Does not include login, purchases, subscriptions, chat, public profiles, gambling, wagering, casino economy, chips, or betting mechanics.
  • Does not actively initialize Google Mobile Ads, preload ads, request ads, show ad views, or show advertising consent prompts while ads are disabled.
  • Does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, Bluetooth, health, or calendar permissions in the current build.
  • Does not expose Sudoku or Air Hockey to users while their feature flags are disabled.
