Privacy choices for Project Game Room.

The controls below use the same structure for every app subsection so users can quickly understand what can be changed, deleted, avoided, or reported.

Local data

  • Use local settings to adjust profiles, colors, sound, and supported gameplay preferences.
  • Delete local app data or delete the app to remove app-local profiles and progress, subject to normal iOS backup behavior.
  • If advertising is enabled in a future build, the website, App Store privacy answers, and in-app privacy choices must be updated before release.

Network and services

  • Support email and ordinary website server logs are handled by the relevant email and hosting providers.

Advertising posture

Advertising-readiness source is present, but ads are disabled by default and Google advertising packages are not linked in the current archive.

Support contact

Email trentshurts@gmail.com with privacy questions.
