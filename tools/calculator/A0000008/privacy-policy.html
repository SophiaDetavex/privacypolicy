1. Information stored locally

  • Workspace hierarchy, notes, editor state, mind-map content, and app preferences.
  • App-local copies of imported images, PDFs, and supported user-selected documents.
  • State needed to restore the local workspace between launches.

2. What may leave the device

  • Files leave the device only when the user deliberately exports, shares, emails, or otherwise sends them through system or third-party services.
  • Support email and ordinary website server logs are handled by the relevant email and hosting providers.

3. Advertising, consent, and purchases

No advertising SDK or active advertising is included in the shipping target.

No account or purchase is required for the disclosed local workspace behavior.

4. What this app does not do

  • Does not require a Project Scribe account.
  • Does not include advertising SDKs or Google AdMob in the shipping target.
  • Does not include first-party analytics, cross-app tracking, or a developer-hosted workspace cloud service.
  • Does not delete original provider files when an imported workspace item is removed from the app.

5. Your choices

  • Delete workspace items in the app or delete the app to remove app-local workspace data, subject to normal iOS backup behavior.
  • Remove imported items from the workspace when you no longer want local app copies.
  • Avoid sending private material by support email unless you are comfortable sharing it through email providers.

6. Contact

For privacy or support questions, email trentshurts@gmail.com.

Change-control requirement

This policy describes the current source package posture. Update this policy, App Store privacy answers, App Review notes, and in-app disclosures before release if app behavior changes.
