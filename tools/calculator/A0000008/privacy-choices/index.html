Controls

Manage data and permissions from the right place.

Some controls live inside the app, some live in iOS Settings, and support-record requests are handled through email.

Delete workspace items

Use in-app controls to remove notes, documents, maps, whiteboards, and imported files where deletion controls are available.

Control file access

Only files you select through Apple pickers are imported. You can avoid importing files and still use note features.

Control image access

PHPicker lets you choose specific images without granting broad Photo Library access. Remove imported images or delete local documents to remove copies.

Remove local app data

Delete the app to remove app-local data managed by iOS, subject to normal system and backup behavior.

Ads and purchases

No ad requests or purchase offers are enabled by default in this updated package. Future monetization requires a new reviewed release and updated disclosures.

Request support or deletion help

Email trentshurts@gmail.com for privacy questions or support-record deletion requests.

Deletion requests

Most app content for this package is local to the device, so the developer generally cannot see or delete it remotely. For support emails or records you sent directly to support, email trentshurts@gmail.com and identify the support conversation you want reviewed.
