Suitability summary

General utility app for users who can safely use calculator and finance-estimation tools. Not a financial adviser, tax adviser, or certified measuring instrument.

Advertising and purchases

Google AdMob banner advertising and Google UMP consent handling are disclosed for the free release. The active ad surface is a fixed 320-by-50-point banner lane, and the app does not request Apple App Tracking Transparency authorization.

No account is required. Purchases are not required for the disclosed calculator workflows in this source package.

Content and interaction

  • Does not require a Great Calculator account.
  • Does not run a developer-hosted calculator-history cloud service.
  • Does not store raw Voice Math audio or raw transcripts in app-controlled storage.
  • Does not ask the user to create an account to use core calculator tools.
