Privacy Choices · A0000006

Privacy choices for Great Calculator.

The controls below use the same structure for every app subsection so users can quickly understand what can be changed, deleted, avoided, or reported.

Local data

  • Delete local history inside the app or remove the app to remove app-local data, subject to normal iOS backup behavior.
  • Use in-app or system privacy controls for advertising consent when available for the user’s region.
  • Do not request live currency rates if you do not want a rate-provider network request.
  • Do not enable microphone or speech workflows if you do not want Apple system voice processing for Voice Math.

Network and services

  • Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform may process advertising, consent, security, diagnostics, performance, and ad-interaction signals depending on region, consent status, and device settings.
  • Frankfurter currency-rate requests may include the selected currency codes when the user requests a live rate; the entered amount is calculated locally.
  • Optional Apple speech or Apple Intelligence features may process voice-to-expression workflows when the user enables and grants those Apple system capabilities.
  • Support email and ordinary website server logs are handled by the relevant email and hosting providers.

Advertising posture

Google AdMob banner advertising and Google UMP consent handling are disclosed for the free release. The active ad surface is a fixed 320-by-50-point banner lane, and the app does not request Apple App Tracking Transparency authorization.

Support contact

Email trentshurts@gmail.com with privacy questions.
