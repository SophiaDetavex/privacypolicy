1. Information stored locally

  • Calibration values, Unit Converter favorites, selected conversion preferences, appearance preferences, and cached validated currency-rate tables.
  • Tool availability and preference state needed to restore local app behavior.

2. What may leave the device

  • When Currency is opened or refreshed, the app requests the latest reference-rate table from the disclosed Frankfurter endpoint; selected pairs and entered amounts are not included in that request.
  • Apple system permissions may provide live camera, microphone, motion, heading, magnetic, torch, and light-related sensor inputs only while relevant tools are active.
  • Support email and ordinary website server logs are handled by the relevant email and hosting providers.

3. Advertising, consent, and purchases

Build 43 keeps Google AdMob dormant: no Google advertising packages, AdMob identifiers, banner requests, or UMP forms are present.

No account, paid content, subscriptions, or in-app purchases are required for the disclosed Build 43 behavior.

4. What this app does not do

  • Does not require a Project Calibrate account.
  • Does not link analytics SDKs, Google advertising SDKs, or Google UMP packages in Build 43.
  • Does not request App Tracking Transparency permission in Build 43.
  • Does not send sensor readings, camera previews, microphone input, selected conversion pairs, entered amounts, favorites, calibration values, or settings to the developer.
  • Does not save photos, videos, or audio recordings from measurement tools.

5. Your choices

  • Use only tools you want to use; permission-gated tools can be denied or disabled through iOS settings.
  • Delete the app to remove app-local preferences, calibration, favorites, and cache data, subject to normal iOS backup behavior.
  • Avoid the Currency tool if you do not want a reference-rate network request.

6. Contact

For privacy or support questions, email trentshurts@gmail.com.
