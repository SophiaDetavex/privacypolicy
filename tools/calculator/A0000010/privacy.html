1. Information kept on the device

  • Tool settings, units, calibration values, theme, onboarding state, and ordinary app preferences.
  • Temporary measurement state needed for active tools; the app does not operate a developer-hosted measurement cloud.
  • A limited local cache of exchange-rate reference data when the currency tool is used.

2. Information that may be processed outside the app

  • Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform may process IP-address-derived general location, device or advertising identifiers, advertising data, product interactions, diagnostic/crash information, and performance data. The exact processing depends on consent, region, Google configuration, device settings, and the ad served.
  • The developer does not receive the app's private workspace or tool content through the advertising integration. Google and its advertising partners process their own service data under their applicable terms and privacy disclosures.
  • When the user requests live currency reference data, the app contacts the Frankfurter service at api.frankfurter.dev. Selected currency codes and ordinary network metadata may be processed by that service; calculations remain local.
  • Camera, microphone, and motion samples are used on-device for the active tool after permission. The audited app does not upload those raw sensor streams to a developer backend.
  • Support email and ordinary website hosting logs are handled by the relevant providers only when those services are used.

3. Advertising and consent

Build 44 integrates one compact Google AdMob banner in a top, centered normal-flow lane and Google UMP consent flow. Invalid, demonstration, blank, or cross-publisher identifiers fail closed; the initial layout is stable while consent is unresolved; and the banner lane collapses after a definitive block or revocation.

4. Permissions and protected resources

  • Camera: on-demand visual measurement, level, or light-related tools after an explicit system grant.
  • Microphone: on-demand sound-related measurement tools after an explicit system grant.
  • Motion: on-demand orientation, level, or movement tools after an explicit system grant.

5. Retention and deletion

App-local records remain until the user deletes them with an available app control, resets the applicable feature, or removes the app, subject to normal device backups and exported copies. Service providers retain data under their own policies and legal obligations. The developer cannot directly erase provider-controlled records that it does not possess; users should use the provider, device, and in-app privacy controls described here.

6. Your choices

  • Decline camera, microphone, or motion permission and continue using tools that do not require the declined capability.
  • Open Ad Privacy Choices to review or change Google consent choices when available.
  • Avoid live currency reference requests to avoid a rate-provider network request.
  • Reset supported calibration values/preferences or remove the app to delete app-local state, subject to normal iOS backup behavior.

7. What the reviewed release does not do

  • No Project Calibrate account or developer-operated measurement cloud is required.
  • Raw camera, microphone, or motion streams are not supplied to Google for advertising through the app's integration.
  • Sensors are not accessed before the user invokes a relevant tool and grants permission.
  • The app does not itself request App Tracking Transparency permission in the audited source package.

8. Children and age suitability

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children through an account or developer backend. Parents and guardians should supervise device permissions, purchases, and service-provider consent as appropriate. See the age-suitability page.

9. Security

The app relies on iOS sandboxing, Apple permission controls, and ordinary platform protections. No software or storage method can guarantee absolute security. Keep iOS current, protect the device passcode, and avoid including sensitive information in support email unless it is necessary.

10. International and provider processing

Apple, Google, email, hosting, and other selected service providers may process data in jurisdictions where they operate. Their own privacy notices govern their processing. The app's first-party local content is not thereby converted into developer-hosted cloud content.

11. Policy changes

A materially changed app or SDK configuration requires this policy and applicable App Store privacy answers to be reviewed before release. The revision date below identifies this published version.
