Advertising privacy choices

Build 44 integrates one compact Google AdMob banner in a top, centered normal-flow lane and Google UMP consent flow. Invalid, demonstration, blank, or cross-publisher identifiers fail closed; the initial layout is stable while consent is unresolved; and the banner lane collapses after a definitive block or revocation.

  1. Open Project Calibrate.
  2. Open Settings or the app's privacy/legal area.
  3. Select Ad Privacy Choices.
  4. Review or change the choices that Google makes available for the user's region.

A control may report that privacy options are not currently required or available. That status can depend on region, consent configuration, network state, and Google UMP. Revoking consent does not necessarily erase data already retained by Google or an advertising partner under its own policy.

Local data controls

  • Decline camera, microphone, or motion permission and continue using tools that do not require the declined capability.
  • Open Ad Privacy Choices to review or change Google consent choices when available.
  • Avoid live currency reference requests to avoid a rate-provider network request.
  • Reset supported calibration values/preferences or remove the app to delete app-local state, subject to normal iOS backup behavior.

iOS permission controls

Protected-resource access can be reviewed in the iOS Settings app. Disabling a permission prevents future access but does not automatically delete content the user previously exported or data retained by an external provider.

  • Camera: on-demand visual measurement, level, or light-related tools after an explicit system grant.
  • Microphone: on-demand sound-related measurement tools after an explicit system grant.
  • Motion: on-demand orientation, level, or movement tools after an explicit system grant.

Support and deletion requests

Because the reviewed app does not require a developer account or upload its private workspace to a developer backend, the developer generally has no server-side app account to delete. For a privacy question or suspected discrepancy, email trentshurts@gmail.com and avoid sending private app content unless necessary.
