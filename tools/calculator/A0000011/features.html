Product information · A0000011

Great Chess features

A focused premium chess experience with local play, computer opponents, lessons, and saved games.

Local-firstUser-controllediPhone and iPad

Core capabilities

  • Local two-player chess and computer opponents with selectable difficulty.
  • Legal move enforcement, castling, en passant, promotion, draw rules, undo, hints, analysis, saved games, and statistics.
  • Interactive lessons and both 2D and procedurally rendered 3D presentation.
  • Premium up-front distribution with no advertising or in-app purchase in the audited release.

Release boundaries

  • No Great Chess account, login, advertising, analytics, or developer cloud is required.
  • No public profiles, chat, matchmaking, real-money wagering, or loot boxes are included.
  • Saved games and statistics are not uploaded to the developer.
  • No protected hardware permission is requested by the audited app target.

Version authority

Source-aligned review completed against Great Chess 1.0 build 1. The unverified third-party USDZ asset was removed and replaced with original procedural SceneKit geometry in the remediated package.
