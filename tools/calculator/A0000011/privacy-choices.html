Great Chess privacy choices

Practical controls for local data, permissions, provider processing, and support.

In-app controlsiOS permissionsDeletion guidance

Advertising privacy choices

The reviewed release does not include active advertising or an advertising-consent SDK, so no in-app ad privacy control is required for that release profile.

Local data controls

  • Delete individual saved games, reset supported statistics/settings, or remove the app to delete app-local data, subject to normal iOS backup behavior.
  • Choose local player/computer modes and 2D or 3D presentation without creating an account.
  • Open external legal or support links only when desired.

iOS permission controls

Protected-resource access can be reviewed in the iOS Settings app. Disabling a permission prevents future access but does not automatically delete content the user previously exported or data retained by an external provider.

  • The audited app target does not declare camera, microphone, speech, location, Contacts, Photos, Calendar, motion, Bluetooth, Health, or local-network usage descriptions.

Support and deletion requests

Because the reviewed app does not require a developer account or upload its private workspace to a developer backend, the developer generally has no server-side app account to delete. For a privacy question or suspected discrepancy, email trentshurts@gmail.com and avoid sending private app content unless necessary.
