Project Milepost Privacy Policy

Project Milepost stores personal milestones locally, accesses Calendar and Photos only when chosen, and may show one consent-gated Google AdMob banner using Google Limited Ads without Apple-defined cross-app tracking.

1. Information stored locally

  • Event names, dates, notes, bundled-history selections, settings, and timeline preferences.
  • Selected Calendar event information imported by the user.
  • App-optimized local copies of selected event photos.
  • Widget snapshot information for the selected local milestone.

2. What may leave the device

  • Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform may process IP-address-derived coarse location, device identifiers, advertising data, product interaction, and performance data for limited banner ad delivery, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and service performance. These are the five data categories disclosed for this release.
  • Build 68 disables Google Mobile Ads SDK exception reporting, publisher personalization, and publisher first-party ID, and forces Google Limited Ads before SDK startup.
  • Milestone text, notes, Calendar records, selected photos, and widget content are not supplied to Google through the advertising integration.
  • Calendar and photo information is accessed through Apple system permissions only when the user chooses those import or selection workflows.
  • Support email and ordinary website server logs are handled by the relevant email and hosting providers.

3. Advertising, consent, tracking, and purchases

Google UMP consent information is refreshed before an eligible ad request. Users can open Menu > Ads > Ad Privacy Choices when Google provides a privacy-options form for the current region and configuration.

The App Store privacy disclosure lists Coarse Location, Device ID, Product Interaction, Advertising Data, and Performance Data as collected by the Google advertising integration, but none is marked as used to track users across other companies' apps or websites. Because this release does not perform Apple-defined tracking and contains no first-party IDFA access or ATT request path, it does not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt.

No in-app purchases, subscriptions, paid content, or ad-removal purchase path are offered in this release.

4. What this app does not do

  • Does not require a Project Milepost account.
  • Does not run a developer server for personal event records.
  • Does not offer in-app purchases, paid content, subscriptions, or ad removal in this release.
  • Does not include advertising or purchase controls inside the Home Screen widget.

5. Your choices

  • Delete events, notes, and local photos in the app or delete the app to remove app-local data, subject to normal iOS backup behavior.
  • Use Menu > Ads > Ad Privacy Choices for available advertising privacy controls.
  • Use Menu > Ads > Report an Ad to report inappropriate or age-inappropriate advertising.
  • Do not use Calendar import or local event photos if you do not want the app to access those user-selected sources.

6. Contact

For privacy or support questions, email trentshurts@gmail.com.

Change-control requirement
