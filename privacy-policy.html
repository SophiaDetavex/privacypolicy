A0000006 · 1.3.3 (270)

Great Calculator

Active consent-aware ads

Great Calculator keeps calculations, saved history, and preferences on the device; uses network access only for selected service-backed features; and integrates a consent-aware Google AdMob banner in the free release.

Advertising: The free release integrates one Google AdMob banner and Google's UMP consent flow. Ad requests are fail-closed when consent or production identifiers do not permit serving. An ad-free purchase is not represented by this source package.
